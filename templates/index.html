BEFORE YOU RELY ON IT

What this check can tell you

This prototype is trained on SMS spam and smishing datasets. Smishing examples are grouped into the model’s “spam” class; it does not return a separate smishing label and may miss other kinds of fraud.

Keep your guard up

A clean result does not make a link or payment request safe. Don’t paste passwords, one-time codes, or sensitive personal details. Verify urgent requests independently.
